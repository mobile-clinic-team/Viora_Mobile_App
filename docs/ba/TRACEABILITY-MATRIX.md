## Coverage notes

- Current Android screens and repository components are based on the inspected source tree.
- Future API routes and database/provider boundaries are taken from `docs/API-SPEC.md`, `docs/DATA-STORAGE.md`, and `docs/AI-ASSISTANT-SPEC.md`; they are target design evidence, not evidence of a deployed service, persistent database, authentication system, or real AI integration.
- Survey cells are intentionally pending until genuine responses exist.
- The final-course MVP includes the real API, persistent database CRUD, authentication, authorization, and one real AI use case. Their implementation and verification remain planned or not started.
- No traceability row claims production database, authentication, AI, audit, or clinical validation evidence.
