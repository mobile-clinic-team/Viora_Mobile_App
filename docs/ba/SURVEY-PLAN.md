# Real user survey plan

## Objective

Collect real evidence about clinic-team workflow pain points, mobile usefulness, desired scheduling/patient-context features, and trust expectations for advisory AI. The survey will validate or challenge the product hypothesis; it will not be used to manufacture support for Viora.

## Respondents

- **Required practical audience:** people familiar with appointment or patient-service workflows, such as clinic reception staff, healthcare administration students, medical students, nurses, or people who have coordinated outpatient visits.
- **Preferred audience:** at least three respondents with direct clinic operations or clinical experience, if realistically reachable.
- **Minimum target:** 15 complete responses.
- **Preferred target:** 20–30 complete responses.
- **Actual completed sample:** 61 recorded responses, exceeding the minimum target. The Q1 consent chart explicitly shows 60 responses; the repository does not claim consent for the 61st response without a raw export. Whether the preferred direct-clinic representation was achieved is not claimed from the available screenshots.
- Respondents who cannot access healthcare workflows may answer a short familiarity question; their responses must be analyzed separately rather than presented as clinic-worker evidence.

## Recruitment and collection

Recruit through the student’s genuine university, professional, or personal network. Use a neutral invitation that states the academic purpose, approximate three-to-five-minute duration, voluntary participation, and anonymous/no-PHI requirement. Do not claim a clinic partnership unless one exists and is documented.

Use Google Forms with response collection configured according to the student’s privacy rules. Do not collect names, email addresses, patient identifiers, employer names, medical histories, or identifiable quotations. The form should allow skipping optional free text and should not require a Google account unless the student has a documented reason.

## Consent and privacy

The opening text must state that participation is voluntary, respondents may stop, responses are for a university product-research assignment, no patient or confidential workplace information should be entered, and aggregated results may appear in the report. Store the form and export in the student’s approved private location. Share only anonymized aggregate results.

## Analysis plan

1. Record total complete responses and excluded/incomplete responses.
2. Report respondent context counts without identifying people or workplaces.
3. Summarize Likert distributions using counts and percentages calculated from the actual response export.
4. Report rankings by score method documented before analysis.
5. Group free-text themes with the number of responses supporting each theme; preserve uncertainty where sample size is small.
6. Derive, change, or reject requirements using the evidence rather than selecting only favorable answers.
7. State sample limitations, recruitment bias, and whether preferred clinician representation was achieved.

## Evidence to preserve

- Final question form screenshot/PDF showing the neutral introduction and questions.
- Response count screenshot from Google Forms.
- Screenshots or exported charts for respondent context, top problems, desired features, mobile constraints, AI trust, and privacy expectations.
- An anonymized CSV or summary table retained privately and referenced by date.
- A dated analysis note linking findings to requirement IDs.
