# UX gaps and usability review

Review basis: current Compose source, `SCREEN-SPEC.md`, `USER-FLOWS.md`, Material theme, and the completed survey. No device usability test was performed in this phase.

| UX ID | Screen | Problem | Severity | Survey evidence | Recommended improvement | Effort | Rubric impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| UX-001 | All final-demo reads | Error, empty, denied, unavailable, and unknown states are implemented through different components and copy patterns. | HIGH | Q14: 57/61 (93.4%) preferred some retry mechanism. | Normalize a shared state panel with title, explanation, retry/review action, and request ID where safe. | M | 2.2, 2.6 |
| UX-002 | Clinical record | `ClinicalState.Error` tells the user to return/reopen but does not expose a direct retry action. | HIGH | Q14 retry preference. | Add a safe retry action if the current context remains valid; otherwise explain why reopen is required. | S | 2.2, 2.6 |
| UX-003 | Appointment create/edit | Date and time are free-form text fields, increasing formatting and timezone error risk. | HIGH | Q4 schedule difficulty 31/61 (50.8%). | Keep the current contract but provide a date/time picker or structured input with explicit timezone/offset confirmation in the final live path. | M | 2.2, 2.4 |
| UX-004 | Appointment detail | Appointment status is mainly text/color and action availability is not summarized before the action list. | MEDIUM | Q4/Q7/Q11 appointment priorities. | Add a labeled status chip and a short “available actions” explanation; retain server allowlists. | S | 2.2, 2.6 |
| UX-005 | Patient detail | Related appointment and encounter content is embedded below the patient projection and may be difficult to scan on a small phone. | MEDIUM | Q11 patient search 28/61 (45.9%), records 26/61 (42.6%). | Use labeled sections or compact cards with independent loading/error states and clear entry actions. | S | 2.2 |
| UX-006 | Doctor directory | Doctor rows use a raw clickable column and have less visual structure than shared `ActionRow` rows. | MEDIUM | Q11 doctor/schedule 24/61 (39.3%). | Normalize doctor rows to the same card/row component and verify the 48dp target. | S | 2.2, 2.4 |
| UX-007 | Role picker | Role buttons are not full-width and do not visually distinguish the selected/active choice before transition. | MEDIUM | Q11 login/authorization 23/61 (37.7%); Q18 60/61 (98.4%) rated authorization 4 or 5. | Use full-width selectable role rows with explicit role descriptions and a clear next action. | S | 2.2 |
| UX-008 | Clinical pages | A top-level `Return` button and the shell back affordance can duplicate navigation choices. | LOW | None direct. | Choose one primary back pattern per final frame and reserve a visible in-content back action for flows where system back is insufficient. | XS | 2.2 |
| UX-009 | Assistant draft | The synthetic generator label and human-review boundary are present, but the final live design needs a compact visual distinction between source, generated text, and reviewer edits. | HIGH | Q15: 53/61 (86.9%) rated AI usefulness 4 or 5; Q17 context/incorrectness concerns. | Define an AI draft card with provenance, uncertainty, current/proposed comparison, reviewer controls, and explicit acceptance state. | M | 2.2, 2.5 |
| UX-010 | Forms and recovery | Saved, rejected, conflict, and outcome-unknown feedback appears as plain text in some flows. | HIGH | Q14: 93.4% preferred retry/recovery. | Use a consistent result panel and place the next safe action next to the message. | S | 2.2, 2.6 |
| UX-011 | Bottom navigation | Four tabs are clear, but the Assistant tab is permission-filtered and there is no visible explanation when it is absent. | MEDIUM | Q18 authorization expectation. | Document or show role-specific navigation explanation without exposing restricted features. | XS | 2.2, 2.4 |
| UX-012 | Patient/admin shells | Preserved dirty-worktree shells add personas and navigation not yet approved in the smallest MVP. | HIGH | Scope governance, not survey evidence. | Decide ownership and final scope before using these shells in Figma or report screenshots. | S | 2.2, 2.7 |

Only UX-001, UX-002, UX-003, UX-009, and UX-010 are recommended for pre-submission implementation review. No fix is implemented in this audit phase.
